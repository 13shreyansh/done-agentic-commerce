Buying one everyday item still means searching, comparing, checking constraints, paying, and trusting a black box. DONE turns that process into one bounded message.

I ask: find twenty no-added-sugar kopi sachets, deliver them to my saved SMU address, and keep the total under ten dollars.

DONE understands the request, then asks once for permission to spend up to my exact ceiling. It does nothing until I reply YES.

Now watch the live evidence console. DONE fetches the real Shopify catalogue, fingerprints the response, rejects anything that breaks my requirements, and explains why the winner is best.

It pays the selected total through StraitsX x402. The authorization is signed locally and the test-XSGD transfer is confirmed on Avalanche Fuji.

AWS Lambda validates the evidence. DynamoDB stores it idempotently. CloudWatch and X-Ray make the execution inspectable.

Finally, iMessage returns the selected product, real cart, Snowtrace proof, and AWS request ID. The sponsor card is sandbox-only, so we prepare the cart without falsely claiming a physical purchase.

DONE. Don’t buy products. Buy outcomes.
